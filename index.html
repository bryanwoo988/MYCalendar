        Data sources

          Public holidays for 2026 and 2027 are copied from the Prime Minister's Department (BKPP) lists. Later years are estimates calculated from the same rules (lunar, Hijri and Tamil calendars) and are labelled "estimated". Replacement days follow section 3 of the Holidays Act 1951 where it applies automatically; Kedah, Kelantan, Terengganu, Sabah and Sarawak announce theirs separately. School holidays are from the KPM circulars. Pay dates are from secondary summaries of JANM circulars.
